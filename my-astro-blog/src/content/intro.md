<b>Q: What does "rakugaki" mean?</b> 

<b>A:</b> the word "rakugaki", at its most literal level, suggests writing that has fallen somewhere, marks that appear without ceremony or permission 

its most well-known and widely-used meaning (today) is to scrawl; to scribble; to graffiti; or to doodle.
